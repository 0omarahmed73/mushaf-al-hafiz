## Quran Text Integrity

- [ ] CHK001 - Is «the words the Mushaf shows» defined precisely enough to check (same location keys, same count, same order, end marker excluded), rather than left as an intuition? [Clarity, Spec §FR-008, research D1]
- [ ] CHK002 - Is it stated that the glyph-level equality of a QCF V2 word and its Unicode word cannot be proven by data, and what replaces that proof (id equality, letter-level cross-check, the owner's by-hand check of 10 ayahs)? [Assumption, research D1, Spec §SC-002]
- [ ] CHK003 - Is the single accepted difference of the independent cross-check (2:72, the hamza written as a letter) recorded with its reason, and is the rule for any new difference (build fails until reviewed) written down? [Completeness, contracts/assets.md]
- [ ] CHK004 - Is it specified what happens to the hizb sign «۞» and the sajda sign «۩» that sit inside some words when a shared text is built (kept unchanged vs dropped)? [Gap, Spec §FR-007]
- [ ] CHK005 - Are the rules for the tafsir text transformation (HTML tags removed, quote marks kept, line breaks kept) stated as the only changes allowed to a human work, and is it clear the Quran words are never transformed? [Clarity, contracts/assets.md, Constitution I]
- [ ] CHK006 - Is the treatment of the 59 ayahs with no commentary of their own (nearest earlier entry plus a note) consistent with the spec's promise that no ayah shows «not found»? [Consistency, Spec §FR-013, AS 2.4, research D2]
- [ ] CHK007 - Is the requirement that tafsir, translation and meanings are never mixed into the shared Quran text defined for all three share paths (ayah, tafsir, translation)? [Consistency, Spec §FR-007, FR-010a, FR-012]

- [ ] CHK030 - Is the decision gate for upgrading the PDF to vector (and its cost: new dependency, owner approval) stated, so a raster PDF is an explicit choice and not an omission? [Assumption, research D7]
