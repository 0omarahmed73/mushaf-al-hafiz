# Look Requirements Quality Checklist: Night Reader Look

**Purpose**: Validate the quality of the requirements in the spec, plan and contracts of feature 004 (a second, Night Reader look chosen in Settings) before tasks are generated: text and layout integrity in both looks, Emerald pixel-identity, contrast, shapes, picture and PDF, the Settings control, start-up and live switching, backup, wird colours, Arabic strings, coordination and test-first coverage. Standard reviewer gate, for the author and the owner at review.
**Created**: 2026-11-20
**Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md), [plan.md](../plan.md), [contracts/](../contracts/)

**Note**: This custom checklist is generated by the `/speckit-checklist` command based on feature context and requirements.
**Review Ownership**: This checklist is a reviewer-owned requirements-quality review artifact. Mark an item `[x]` only when the reviewer determines the requirements-quality criterion is satisfied.
**Marker Semantics**: `[x]` means the criterion has been reviewed and satisfied for requirements quality. It does not mean implementation work is complete.

## Quran Text and Layout Integrity (Constitution I and II)

- [ ] CHK001 Is it stated, as a requirement and not only as a plan intent, that a look changes only colours, borders, radii, markers and the page frame, and never text, order, line distribution or geometry? [Completeness, Spec §FR-008, Plan Constitution Check I]
- [ ] CHK002 Is "no ivory card and no gold frame" in Night reconciled with "the same box and the same lines" by a measurable rule (same paddings and border width, the frame drawn transparent), rather than by the word "frame-less" alone? [Clarity, Spec §FR-007, Research D3]
- [ ] CHK003 Can "the same lines, words and line breaks in both looks" be objectively verified, including the sampled-versus-all-604-pages policy and what is measured (line boxes and word boxes, not only word counts)? [Measurability, Spec §SC-004, Research D3]
- [ ] CHK004 Is the derivation of the ayah-end markers fully defined (the rule, the last word of a page, the expected total of 6,236) and is the fallback when it proves unreliable stated together with who decides? [Completeness, Research D3, Data Model]
- [ ] CHK005 Is it clear that the end-marker derivation adds no data file, changes no asset and leaves the stored page JSON untouched (FR-017 versus a derived set)? [Consistency, Spec §FR-017, Data Model]
- [ ] CHK006 Are the behaviours that must stay identical in Night (position saving, page-turn direction, tap, long press with highlight, pinch zoom, feature 003's text size and panning) listed as one set with an acceptance scenario each? [Coverage, Spec §FR-009, §US2]
- [ ] CHK007 Is the Night highlight of a long-pressed ayah defined by a measurable property (a colour and its contrast against the dark page and the ink), not only as "clearly visible"? [Ambiguity, Spec §FR-009, Data Model]
- [ ] CHK008 Is the bottom bar's floating form required to keep the page area and feature 003's page-box base unchanged, with the slot height stated? [Completeness, Research D5, Spec §FR-006]

## Emerald Identity and Unchanged Tests

- [ ] CHK009 Is "Emerald stays exactly as today" defined by a verifiable rule (every token equals its existing constant or literal, today's theme unchanged, every earlier test passes unchanged) rather than by "looks the same"? [Measurability, Spec §FR-002, §SC-003, Data Model]
- [ ] CHK010 Is the fallback that makes old tests keep their colours (a theme without the look extension reads Emerald) written as a requirement of the contract and not only as an implementation habit? [Gap, Contract dart-interfaces `LookContext`]

## Contrast and Colour Measurability (FR-012, SC-005)
